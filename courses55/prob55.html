Overview

What the course is

Analytic number theory has become probabilistic: random Euler products, random multiplicative functions and log-correlated fields now carry many of the main results about ζ\zetaζ. Math 55 does not even cover measure theory, so this is the largest gap for a number theorist in that direction.

Prob 55a should run alongside ANT 55a, because the probabilistic half of ANT 55b assumes all of Prob 55a.

Assessment

Problem sets, exams and project

Weekly set: 6–8 problems from the text, one problem from the “link to number theory” column, and one simulation when the table calls for it.

  • 55a: the MIT 18.175 problem sets are lists of Durrett problems: use them as they are, then add Durrett's and Vershynin's exercises.
  • 55b: Zeitouni's exercises inside his notes and the exercises at the end of each Berestycki–Powell chapter. In the paper weeks (6–12), rewrite the proof of one main theorem in full detail in 3–4 pages.
  • Exams: two and a half hours, closed book. In 55b, include one problem of the form “explain the proof strategy of paper X on one page”.

Project (weeks 8–13 of 55b)

A 10–15 page paper in one of these forms:

  1. A rigorous tail estimate with explicit constants for the random Euler product, turning a numerical estimate into a theorem.
  2. An exposition of the upper-bound proof in ABR I, suitable as a writing sample for PhD applications.
  3. A probabilistic analysis of a hybrid model (true phases for small primes, random phases for the rest), in the precise language of weeks 4–5 and 11 of 55a.
