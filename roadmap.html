How to use it

Topics are grouped into areas and listed roughly in the order you would learn them. For exact prerequisites, follow the arrows in Talal’s dependency diagram. You don’t need every resource: skim the contents of a few books, watch the first lecture of a few courses, and keep the ones that suit you.
