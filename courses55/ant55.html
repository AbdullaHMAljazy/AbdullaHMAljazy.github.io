ANT 55: Analytic Number Theory

From arithmetic functions to the large values of ζ(1+it)\zeta(1+it)ζ(1+it)

Two terms plus a week zero, with the shape and pace of Math 55 and the material of the first two years of a PhD in analytic number theory. It ends on purpose at the large values of ζ(1+it)\zeta(1+it)ζ(1+it) and the random Euler product model of Granville and Soundararajan.

Overview

What the course is

The course keeps everything that makes Math 55 work: a weekly problem set that carries about two thirds of the weight, two midterms and a final each term, and a steady pace. Because it is at graduate level it is heavier in content than Math 55 itself, but it builds on analysis the reader already has.

Assessment

Problem sets, exams and project

Each weekly set has 8–10 problems: 6–8 proofs written in LaTeX, one computational problem from the table, and one “read a paper” problem (summarise a proof from a recent paper in one page).

  • Problem sources: Ben Green's C3.8 problem sheets (some with solutions), Silberman's problem sets inside his notes, the exercises scattered through Tao's 254A notes, and the exercises of Ford and Harper.
  • Exams: two and a half hours, closed book: two theorem statements with one proof, two estimates (for example deriving an explicit formula with error terms), and one open-ended problem.
  • Outside review: after each midterm, show one solution to a number theorist and ask for feedback on the write-up.

Capstone (weeks 9–13 of 55b)

A 15–20 page paper in one of these forms:

  1. A complete, self-contained exposition of Granville and Soundararajan's distribution result for ΦT(τ)\Phi_T(\tau)ΦT​(τ), with every estimate the paper leaves implicit written out.
  2. A research paper or draft with a supervisor, whose theoretical part is built on the tools of weeks 8–12.
  3. A survey of large values of L(1,χ)L(1,\chi)L(1,χ) compared with ζ(1+it)\zeta(1+it)ζ(1+it), suitable as a writing sample for PhD applications.

If research on the values of ζ\zetaζ is your goal, do not wait for 55a to end: read weeks 8–12 of 55b alongside it from the start.
