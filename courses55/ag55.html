AG 55: Algebraic Geometry towards Homological Mirror Symmetry

Schemes and cohomology, then derived categories, Fukaya categories and HMS for the elliptic curve

Two terms in the Math 55 rhythm. The first is a standard first course on schemes and their cohomology, built on Vakil's freely posted drafts; the second goes from derived categories and A∞A_\inftyA∞​-algebras through Lagrangian Floer theory to the Fukaya category, and ends by working through Kontsevich's conjecture for the elliptic curve.

Overview

What the course is

Ravi Vakil wrote The Rising Sea in public and still posts drafts of it; the first term follows its chapters, with Gathmann's notes as a gentler companion and MIT 18.726 for cohomology. The second term is assembled from survey notes by the people who built the subject: Căldăraru on derived categories, Keller on A∞A_\inftyA∞​-algebras, Cannas da Silva on symplectic geometry, Auroux and Smith on Fukaya categories, and the original papers of Kontsevich and Polishchuk–Zaslow.

Assessment

Problem sets, exams and project

Weekly set: 8–10 problems. Vakil's book is famous for its exercises; in the first term, take most problems from it.

  • Problem sources: Vakil's exercises (many courses assign them directly), the MIT 18.726 problem sets, Gathmann's exercises, Keller's problems, and the homework in Cannas da Silva.
  • Exams: two and a half hours, closed book; in 55b include one problem that computes morphisms in a Fukaya or derived category of a curve.

Project

A 10–15 page exposition of one of the following:

  1. HMS for the elliptic curve, written out completely with every identity of theta functions checked.
  2. Beilinson's description of Db(Pn)D^b(\mathbb{P}^n)Db(Pn) and its mirror.
  3. A survey of derived equivalences of K3 surfaces.
